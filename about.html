About North Star Bakery

Story of the Bakery

North Star Bakery was born out of a desire to bring real, unadulterated bread back to our neighborhood tables. In 2018, head baker Elena Rostova transformed a former historic storefront in Portland, Maine, into a dedicated stone-hearth bakery. What began as a one-woman morning stall has expanded into a warm, community-centered meeting space where neighbors, commuters, and families gather over crusty loaves and espresso.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that exceptional bread begins in healthy soil. Our sourcing philosophy is grounded in transparency, sustainability, and regional agriculture:

  • 100% Regional Organic Grains: We partner with independent New England farmers who grow organic hard red spring wheat, heritage rye, and spelt without synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilizers.
  • Stone-Ground Freshness: Whole grains are stone-milled to retain the nutrient-rich germ and aromatic bran oils that commercial roller mills strip away.
  • Pure Ingredients: Our traditional loaves contain only four elements: organic stone-ground flour, filtered water, unrefined Maine sea salt, and our living wild starter culture.
  • Zero Artificial Additives: We never use dough conditioners, bleaching agents, preservatives, or artificial flavor enhancers.

Staff Highlights & Baking Team

Our close-knit baking team works in quiet coordination every morning while the city sleeps:

  • Elena Rostova (Founder & Head Baker): Elena learned classical sourdough fermentation while working in traditional village bakeries across France and Vermont. She oversees dough hydration, wild starters, and stone-oven baking schedules.
  • Marcus Vance (Pastry Chef & Lamination Specialist): Marcus brings a passion for French viennoiserie, crafting our 27-layer butter croissants and seasonal fruit tarts each day.
  • Amara Lin (Front of House & Community Pre-Order Lead): Amara greets our morning customers, coordinates catering boxes, and manages custom pre-orders for local families and events.
