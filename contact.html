Inquiry and Pre-Order Guidelines

Because all of our sourdough breads undergo a slow 24 to 36-hour wild fermentation, daily baking quantities are limited. Submitting an advance request guarantees that your favorite loaves and pastry boxes are reserved and packed fresh for you. Please submit all pre-orders at least 24 hours in advance (48 hours for specialty cakes). We will confirm your request via email within 2 business hours.
